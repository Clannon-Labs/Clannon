# Skill: structuring a brief

Write briefs that a busy reader can act on:

- Open with a 1-2 sentence bottom-line answer, then support it.
- Use short sections or bullets; lead each with the point, then the detail.
- Attribute load-bearing claims to their source inline, and collect the URLs in
  citations.
- Flag uncertainty plainly ("evidence is mixed", "as of <date>") rather than
  overstating confidence.
- Keep it tight: cut anything that doesn't change the reader's understanding.
