# Skill: evaluating sources

When researching the open web, judge each source before relying on it:

- Prefer primary sources, official docs, and reputable outlets over aggregators
  and content farms.
- Note the publication date; for fast-moving topics, prefer recent material and
  say when something may be outdated.
- Cross-check any surprising or load-bearing claim against a second independent
  source before stating it as fact.
- Separate facts (supported by a source) from inferences (your reasoning) and
  make that distinction explicit in the findings.
- Always keep the URL of anything you cite so it can go in the citations list.
