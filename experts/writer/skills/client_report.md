# Skill: structuring a client research report

For synthesizing multiple research findings into a report a client pays for:

- Title, then **Executive summary**: 3-5 sentences a client could forward as-is —
  the answer, the strongest evidence, the recommendation.
- One section per research angle (use the findings' natural seams, not one
  section per source). Lead each section with its takeaway in bold, then the
  supporting detail.
- **Recommendations / next steps**: numbered, concrete, ordered by impact. Tie
  each to the evidence section it rests on.
- **Sources & confidence**: list the sources actually relied on; note where
  findings disagreed or coverage was thin instead of papering over it.
- Markdown throughout (`##` sections, bold leads, tables only for genuinely
  tabular comparisons). Aim for completeness over brevity — this is the
  deliverable, not a chat answer — but never pad.
- Reconcile the findings: if two angles overlap or conflict, say which to trust
  and why. Never paste findings end-to-end.
